This batch of songs is highly experimental, blending raw, heavy textures with elements you wouldn't expect — like the dark-jazz saxophone in Ekstaas or the solemn, choral chanting in Metalne Maastik. It feels less like a traditional metal project and more like avant-garde music that uses heavy distortion as just one tool in its emotional toolbox.
